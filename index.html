Politika privatnosti

Važi od 11. oktobra 2026.

Ukratko: Rokko ne prikuplja, ne šalje i ne prodaje vaše podatke. Sve što unesete ostaje na vašem telefonu. Aplikacija nema nalog, nema server, nema reklame i nema praćenje.

Šta aplikacija radi

Rokko je podsetnik na rokove koji ističu (registracija vozila, osiguranje, lična dokumenta, garancije, pretplate…). Obaveštenja se zakazuju na samom telefonu i stižu i bez interneta.

Podaci koje unosite

Nazivi podsetnika, datumi, napomene, „čije/šta“, mesto, stavke čekliste, iznosi obnove i fotografije dokumenata koje sami dodate čuvaju se isključivo u privatnoj memoriji aplikacije na vašem uređaju. Mi (autor aplikacije) nemamo pristup tim podacima i ne možemo da ih vidimo.

Dozvole koje aplikacija traži i zašto

  • Obaveštenja – da bi podsetnici mogli da stignu.
  • Kamera i galerija – samo kad sami izaberete da dodate fotografiju dokumenta. Fotografija se kopira u privatni folder aplikacije i nigde se ne šalje.
  • Alarmi i podsetnici (tačno vreme) – po želji, da obaveštenje stigne tačno u minut.
  • Pokretanje posle restarta telefona – da zakazana obaveštenja ostanu zakazana i posle restarta.

Deljenje podataka

Podaci napuštaju telefon samo kad vi to izaberete: „Podeli rezervnu kopiju“ ili „Izvezi u PDF“ prave fajl i prosleđuju ga aplikaciji koju vi odaberete (mejl, Google Drive, Viber…). Od tog trenutka za fajl važe pravila te aplikacije. „Otvori u mapama“ prosleđuje samo tekst mesta koje ste upisali aplikaciji za mape na vašem telefonu.

Ako je na telefonu uključena Android rezervna kopija (Google backup), Android sam čuva podatke aplikacije na vašem Google nalogu i vraća ih pri ponovnoj instalaciji. To radi Google po svojoj politici privatnosti; mi nemamo pristup tim podacima.

Kupovina, treće strane, analitika i reklame

Aplikacija ne sadrži reklame, analitiku ni alate za praćenje i ne šalje nikakve podatke trećim stranama. Aplikacija nudi jednokratnu kupovinu „Pro zauvek“ (besplatna verzija ima do 4 aktivna podsetnika). Plaćanje u celosti obrađuje Google Play po sopstvenoj politici privatnosti; mi ne dobijamo podatke o vašoj kartici ni o vama, a aplikacija na telefonu pamti samo da je Pro aktivan.

Deca

Aplikacija nije namenjena deci mlađoj od 13 godina i ne prikuplja podatke ni od koga.

Brisanje podataka

Deinstalacija aplikacije briše sve njene podatke sa telefona (kopiju na Google nalogu, ako postoji, brišete u podešavanjima Google naloga). Rezervne kopije i PDF fajlove koje ste sami sačuvali ili poslali brišete tamo gde ste ih stavili.

Promene ove politike

Ako se politika promeni, nova verzija će biti objavljena na ovoj stranici sa novim datumom.

Privacy Policy

Effective 11 October 2026.

In short: Rokko does not collect, transmit or sell your data. Everything you enter stays on your phone only. There is no account, no server, no ads and no tracking.

What the app does

Rokko reminds you of things that expire (vehicle registration, insurance, ID documents, warranties, subscriptions…). Notifications are scheduled on the phone itself and arrive even without an internet connection.

Data you enter

Reminder names, dates, notes, “whose / what”, place, checklist items, renewal amounts and photos of documents you add yourself are stored exclusively in the app’s private storage on your device. We (the app’s author) have no access to this data and cannot see it.

Permissions and why they are needed

  • Notifications – so reminders can reach you.
  • Camera and photos – only when you choose to add a photo of a document. The photo is copied into the app’s private folder and is never uploaded anywhere.
  • Alarms & reminders (exact time) – optional, so a notification arrives at the exact minute.
  • Run after phone restart – so scheduled notifications survive a reboot.

Sharing

Data leaves the phone only when you choose so: “Share backup” and “Export to PDF” create a file and hand it to the app you pick (email, Google Drive, messaging…). From that moment the file is governed by that app’s rules. “Open in maps” passes only the place text you typed to the maps app on your phone.

If Android backup (Google backup) is turned on on your phone, Android itself keeps the app’s data in your Google account and restores it when the app is reinstalled. This is done by Google under its own privacy policy; we have no access to that data.

Purchases, third parties, analytics and ads

The app contains no ads, no analytics and no tracking tools, and sends no data to third parties. The app offers a one-time “Pro forever” purchase (the free version holds up to 4 active reminders). Payment is processed entirely by Google Play under its own privacy policy; we never receive your card details or any information about you, and the app only stores on your phone that Pro is active.

Children

The app is not intended for children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app deletes all of its data from the phone (a copy in your Google account, if any, is deleted in your Google account settings). Backups and PDF files you saved or sent yourself are deleted wherever you put them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
