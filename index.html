Politika privatnosti

Važi od 5. novembra 2026.

Ukratko: Rokko ne prikuplja i ne prodaje vaše podatke i nikad ih ne šalje nama. Sve što unesete ostaje na vašem telefonu – osim ako sami uključite kopiju na svoj Google Drive. Aplikacija nema nalog kod nas, nema server, nema reklame i nema praćenje.

Šta aplikacija radi

Rokko je podsetnik na rokove koji ističu (registracija vozila, osiguranje, lična dokumenta, garancije, pretplate, rođendani…). Obaveštenja se zakazuju na samom telefonu i stižu i bez interneta.

Podaci koje unosite

Nazivi podsetnika, datumi, napomene, „čije/šta“, mesto, stavke čekliste, iznosi obnove, fotografije i PDF dokumenti koje sami dodate i podešavanja aplikacije čuvaju se isključivo u privatnoj memoriji aplikacije na vašem uređaju. Mi (autor aplikacije) nemamo pristup tim podacima i ne možemo da ih vidimo.

Dozvole koje aplikacija traži i zašto

  • Obaveštenja – da bi podsetnici mogli da stignu.
  • Alarmi i podsetnici (tačno vreme) – po želji, da obaveštenje stigne tačno u minut.
  • Pokretanje posle restarta telefona – da zakazana obaveštenja ostanu zakazana i posle restarta.
  • Otisak prsta ili lice – samo ako uključite „Zaključavanje aplikacije“. Otključavanje radi telefon (otisak, lice ili PIN telefona); Rokko ne vidi i ne čuva otisak, lice ni PIN, nego dobija samo odgovor da li je otključano.
  • Kalendar (čitanje i upis) – samo ako uključite „Upis u kalendar“; traži se tek tada. Aplikacija čita samo spisak kalendara (da biste izabrali gde se upisuje) i svoje događaje, a upisuje naziv, kategoriju, belešku i mesto podsetnika kao celodnevni događaj na dan isteka. Ako izaberete kalendar Google naloga, te događaje sinhronizuje Google po podešavanjima vašeg naloga; Rokko sam ništa ne šalje. Isključivanje upisa briše sve Rokko događaje.
  • Internet – za kupovinu „Pro zauvek“ preko Google Play-a i, ako je uključite, za kopiju na vaš Google Drive (vidi dole). Ništa se ne šalje nama.

Za fotografije i dokumente aplikacija ne traži dozvolu za kameru, galeriju ni fajlove: koristi sistemsku kameru, sistemski birač fotografija i sistemski birač fajlova, pa dobija samo ono što sami napravite ili izaberete. Fotografija ili PDF se kopira u privatni folder aplikacije. Kad otvorite PDF prilog, privremena kopija se prosleđuje aplikaciji za PDF koju izaberete.

Skeniranje pasoša, lične karte ili vozačke dozvole: datum isteka se čita sa fotografije samo na telefonu, bez interneta. Iz pročitanog teksta uzima se datum isteka i, ako to potvrdite („Upiši i ime“), ime – ono se upisuje u polje „Čije/šta“ podsetnika. Prezime, broj dokumenta i datum rođenja se ne čuvaju. Fotografija za skeniranje se posle čitanja briše, osim ako izaberete „Sačuvaj i fotografiju kao prilog“.

Deljenje podataka

Podaci napuštaju telefon samo kad vi to izaberete: „Podeli rezervnu kopiju“ ili „Izvezi u PDF“ prave fajl i prosleđuju ga aplikaciji koju vi odaberete (mejl, Google Drive, Viber…). Od tog trenutka za fajl važe pravila te aplikacije. „Otvori u mapama“ prosleđuje samo tekst mesta koje ste upisali aplikaciji za mape na vašem telefonu.

Kopija na Google Drive (po želji): ako je uključite i prijavite se svojim Google nalogom, aplikacija šalje rezervnu kopiju (podsetnike, podešavanja i priloge) direktno sa telefona u fasciklu „Rokko“ na vašem Google Drive-u – sama, posle izmena, najviše jednom dnevno, i čuva poslednje 3. Rokko dobija dozvolu samo za fajlove koje sam napravi i ne vidi ostatak vašeg Drive-a. Mi nemamo pristup tim kopijama i nemamo server preko kog bi išle. Isključivanje odjavljuje nalog; kopije ostaju na Drive-u dok ih ne obrišete. Prijavu, slanje i čuvanje na Drive-u uređuju Google-ovi uslovi korišćenja i politika privatnosti.

Ako je na telefonu uključena Android rezervna kopija (Google backup), Android sam čuva podatke aplikacije na vašem Google nalogu i vraća ih pri ponovnoj instalaciji. To radi Google po svojoj politici privatnosti; mi nemamo pristup tim podacima.

Kupovina, treće strane, analitika i reklame

Aplikacija ne sadrži reklame, analitiku ni alate za praćenje i ne šalje nikakve podatke trećim stranama. Aplikacija nudi jednokratnu kupovinu „Pro zauvek“ (besplatna verzija ima do 4 aktivna podsetnika). Plaćanje u celosti obrađuje Google Play po sopstvenoj politici privatnosti; mi ne dobijamo podatke o vašoj kartici ni o vama, a aplikacija na telefonu pamti samo da je Pro aktivan.

Deca

Aplikacija nije namenjena deci mlađoj od 13 godina i ne prikuplja podatke ni od koga.

Brisanje podataka

Deinstalacija aplikacije briše njene podatke sa telefona (kopiju na Google nalogu, ako postoji, brišete u podešavanjima Google naloga). Izuzetak je kalendar: ako ste uključili upis u kalendar, Rokko-ov kalendar i njegovi događaji ostaju na telefonu i posle deinstalacije (Android ih ne briše sam) – isključite upis pre deinstalacije ili ih obrišite u aplikaciji za kalendar. Kopije na Google Drive-u, rezervne kopije i PDF fajlove koje ste sami sačuvali ili poslali brišete tamo gde se nalaze.

Promene ove politike

Ako se politika promeni, nova verzija će biti objavljena na ovoj stranici sa novim datumom.

Privacy Policy

Effective 5 November 2026.

In short: Rokko does not collect or sell your data and never sends it to us. Everything you enter stays on your phone – unless you turn on a backup to your own Google Drive. There is no account with us, no server, no ads and no tracking.

What the app does

Rokko reminds you of things that expire (vehicle registration, insurance, ID documents, warranties, subscriptions, birthdays…). Notifications are scheduled on the phone itself and arrive even without an internet connection.

Data you enter

Reminder names, dates, notes, “whose / what”, place, checklist items, renewal amounts, photos and PDF documents you add yourself, and the app’s settings are stored exclusively in the app’s private storage on your device. We (the app’s author) have no access to this data and cannot see it.

Permissions and why they are needed

  • Notifications – so reminders can reach you.
  • Alarms & reminders (exact time) – optional, so a notification arrives at the exact minute.
  • Run after phone restart – so scheduled notifications survive a reboot.
  • Fingerprint or face – only if you turn on “App lock”. Unlocking is done by the phone (fingerprint, face or the phone’s PIN); Rokko never sees or stores your fingerprint, face or PIN – it only receives whether unlocking succeeded.
  • Calendar (read and write) – only if you turn on “Add to calendar”; it is requested only then. The app reads just the list of calendars (so you can choose where to add) and its own events, and writes the reminder’s name, category, note and place as an all-day event on the expiry date. If you choose a Google account calendar, Google syncs those events according to your account settings; Rokko itself sends nothing. Turning it off deletes all Rokko events.
  • Internet – for the “Pro forever” purchase through Google Play and, if you turn it on, for the backup to your Google Drive (see below). Nothing is ever sent to us.

For photos and documents the app does not ask for camera, photo-library or file permission: it uses the system camera, the system photo picker and the system file picker, so it only receives what you take or pick. The photo or PDF is copied into the app’s private folder. When you open a PDF attachment, a temporary copy is handed to the PDF app you choose.

Passport, ID card or driving licence scan: the expiry date is read from the photo on the phone only, without internet. From the recognised text the app takes the expiry date and, if you confirm it (“Also fill in the name”), the first name – it goes into the reminder’s “Whose / what” field. Surname, document number and date of birth are not stored. The scan photo is deleted after reading unless you choose “Also save the photo as an attachment”.

Sharing

Data leaves the phone only when you choose so: “Share backup” and “Export to PDF” create a file and hand it to the app you pick (email, Google Drive, messaging…). From that moment the file is governed by that app’s rules. “Open in maps” passes only the place text you typed to the maps app on your phone.

Backup to Google Drive (optional): if you turn it on and sign in with your Google account, the app sends a backup (reminders, settings and attachments) directly from the phone to a “Rokko” folder in your Google Drive – automatically, after changes, at most once a day – and keeps the last 3. Rokko only gets access to the files it creates itself and cannot see the rest of your Drive. We have no access to these backups and no server they pass through. Turning it off signs the account out; the backups stay in your Drive until you delete them. Sign-in, upload and storage in Drive are governed by Google’s terms of service and privacy policy.

If Android backup (Google backup) is turned on on your phone, Android itself keeps the app’s data in your Google account and restores it when the app is reinstalled. This is done by Google under its own privacy policy; we have no access to that data.

Purchases, third parties, analytics and ads

The app contains no ads, no analytics and no tracking tools, and sends no data to third parties. The app offers a one-time “Pro forever” purchase (the free version holds up to 4 active reminders). Payment is processed entirely by Google Play under its own privacy policy; we never receive your card details or any information about you, and the app only stores on your phone that Pro is active.

Children

The app is not intended for children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app deletes its data from the phone (a copy in your Google account, if any, is deleted in your Google account settings). The exception is the calendar: if you turned on “Add to calendar”, Rokko’s calendar and its events stay on the phone after uninstalling (Android does not remove them) – turn it off before uninstalling or delete them in your calendar app. Backups in Google Drive, and backups and PDF files you saved or sent yourself, are deleted wherever they are.
